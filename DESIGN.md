# Design notes

How the job-search tool works, the decisions behind it, what was tried and rejected, and what is left to improve. Keep this file updated whenever a decision changes.

## Goal

Find every open software-engineering role in India that fits the profile in `profile.json`: level and title, years asked ≤ `max_min_years`, pay ≥ `min_pay_lpa` (₹ LPA), not a services or staffing firm. Rank the roles, and track applications in a dashboard. Read-only: no logins, no applying, nothing personal sent anywhere.

## Architecture

```
tiers.json (company bank, ~1,200)   companies.json (~335 direct sources + 2 LinkedIn sources)   profile.json (criteria)
            │                                   │
            ▼                                   ▼
crawl.py ── fast phase: boards, platform feeds, portals/, 16 at a time (~1 min) ┐
        └─ LinkedIn phase: throttled guest search, in the background ────┤  each source → output/raw/<source>.json
                                                                         ▼
           evaluate_all (no network, ~2 s): resolve company → exclude services/staffing → drop duplicates
           → title/location/contract filters → pay floor → years asked → score → group requisitions
                                                                         ▼
           jobs / leads / rejected / company-bank CSVs, report.py table, build_dashboard.py → dashboard.html
```

Supporting tools: `discover_ats.py` (find direct boards), `coverage.py` (per-company coverage status), `find_careers.py` (find each company's careers site and the platform behind it; see `CAREERS_DISCOVERY.md`), `import_lists.py` (grow the bank from public lists), `refresh.sh` (fast phase, then LinkedIn in the background).

## Key decisions

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Read company job boards directly** (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Amazon, Microsoft, Atlassian, Google) | Complete, current, has the full description, and the apply link is the company's own. A role that disappears from the board has closed. |
| **Read companies' own careers sites** (Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Eightfold, Rippling feeds; `portals/` for custom sites) | A study of 100 board-less companies showed most careers pages load jobs from a public feed. JPMorgan, Apple, Goldman Sachs, Qualcomm, Juspay and others were LinkedIn-only before. Details in `CAREERS_DISCOVERY.md`. |
| **Custom portals: one small module each, plus a configurable job-links reader** | Sites differ too much for one generic scraper, but each needs only ~30 lines. Server-rendered link lists need only config (`portals/links.py`). |
| **Sitemaps + job-page JobPosting data as a generic reader** (`portals/sitemap.py`) | Phenom, iCIMS, Avature, SuccessFactors and many custom sites have no public list feed, but their sitemaps list every job and each job page carries schema.org JobPosting data (for Google for Jobs). One reader covers them all: URLs are filtered by wording before any page is fetched, robots.txt is honoured, and expired `validThrough` dates are dropped. The idea came from strata-harvest (MIT); its "stealth"/"impersonating" fetchers were deliberately not used. A source raises instead of returning nothing when no sitemap can be read, or when a sitemap its jobs come from fails (one named in `companies.json`, or a job sitemap an index points to), so the crawl keeps its previous jobs rather than treating every role as closed; the guessed `/sitemap.xml` may fail, and a sitemap that loads but lists nothing means no openings. One malformed job page is skipped, not fatal. `"india_in_url": true` limits reading to job URLs that name an Indian city, for sites whose URLs always carry the city (Millennium's Eightfold: 26 s → 10 s). |
| **robots.txt read as RFC 9309: the group for our product token (else `*`), longest matching rule wins, Allow wins a tie; no robots.txt (4xx) allows everything, a server error or unreachable host disallows everything for that run** (`portals/sitemap.py` `Robots.load`, also used by `find_careers.py`) | Python's `urllib.robotparser` applies the first matching line, so a file like Eightfold's `Disallow: /` then `Allow: /careers` blocked every job page the site explicitly allows. That alone had kept Millennium (levels.fyi India SE median 68.7 LPA) "blocked"; its sitemap and job pages are public and allowed. Its PCSX search API answers "PCSX is not enabled", a deliberate setting, so it isn't used. |
| **Bare-ID job sitemaps are read through a page cache** (`"cache_pages": true`, `output/sitemap-cache/<company>.json`) | Meta's careers search runs on GraphQL calls that need a per-page token (not used), but its robots.txt lists a public sitemap of every job (~1,350) and each job page carries JobPosting data. The URLs are bare IDs, so the usual filter-by-URL-wording can't pick software roles. Every page is read once (about 5 minutes, 4 at a time), its summary cached, and later runs read only new IDs; an ID gone from the sitemap is a closed job. Non-India descriptions aren't kept. A page that loads without JobPosting data (it may be a block or rate-limit page) is stored as `{"empty": date}` and read again after 7 days, so a one-off block doesn't hide a job for good. |
| **"Blocked" sites get one look in the user's own browser to find the feed the page itself calls; nothing manual is left in the refresh** (11 Nov 2026) | Several sites refuse scripts only on the page, not on the list call behind it: Myntra's portal runs on Spire2Grow (existing reader, workspace `MYNTRA-93as3`), Navi's careers page calls `navi.com/api/careers/jobs` (the TurboHire pass-through format, all jobs in one response), and Zerodha's calls `careers.zerodha.com/api/jobs`. All three answer plain requests and are read every run, eligible roles or not. A saved-page importer was built and then dropped: it made every refresh depend on the user saving pages. |
| **Boards no script can read are searched on LinkedIn every run** (`tiers.json` `"linkedin_every_run"`, with the reason in `board_note`) | A board with no eligible role today is still read every run, so a new opening shows up. Ten well-paying companies have boards that can't be read at all without getting around something: Flipkart and Ola (TurboHire's robots.txt disallows every bot but Googlebot, and its list API needs an auth token), Revolut (bot challenge), Wayfair (PerimeterX), Citadel and Citadel Securities (Cloudflare; their sitemaps give titles but no locations), Best Buy (job pages carry no location; the list API is token-protected), UBS (sign-in), Jio (form postbacks), Hotstar (JioStar Workday 403). These are searched on LinkedIn on every run, on top of the rotating 40-company budget, instead of waiting their turn (Ola, Best Buy, UBS and Jio had never been searched). The flag makes a company a sweep candidate whatever its quality score; it has no effect once the company has a direct source or is found to pay below the bar. Reading the TurboHire page's token from the browser was rejected: that is the token wall. |
| **Small readers for Indian ATS pages: Keka, Zoho Recruit, Kula, PyjamaHR, Zappyhire, Zwayam, RippleHire, TurboHire pass-through feeds; iCIMS Jibe feeds; Jobvite lists; SuccessFactors `sitemal.xml` feeds** | Each careers page loads a public feed or embeds its list. One reader per platform covers dozens of companies that were LinkedIn-only. SuccessFactors sites disallow `/services/` in robots.txt, so their RSS job feed (one request, every job) is read instead of the search API. |
| **RippleHire: the token is read from the company's own careers page on every run, never stored** | RippleHire's search needs the career-site token that each company puts in its published careers link (user agreed 10 Nov 2026). Reading it fresh keeps working if it rotates and keeps tokens out of `companies.json`; job links still carry the token, as the company's own published link does. Banks list thousands of branch roles, so they search `engineer OR developer OR software` (Axis 9,756 -> 88 jobs). |
| **Hand-built readers only for companies whose pay clears the bar** | A custom site needs its own ~30-line reader, so pending companies are pay-checked on levels.fyi first (75 on 10 Nov 2026) and readers are built for good and borderline payers only (Khatabook, MediaTek, PayU, Nomura, DTCC, MiQ, HSBC…). Low payers stay on LinkedIn. |
| **"City, IN" alone is Indiana, not India** | SuccessFactors writes India as "City, IN" or "City, ST, IN", and US sites write Indiana as "City, IN". A two-part "City, IN" counts as India only with another India marker (a known city or state); the three-part form always counts. |
| **Job portals are never taken as an employer's source** | Apna's and GoodSpace's sitemaps list other employers' jobs; reading them would mislabel roles. |
| **Company domains from Clearbit's autocomplete, careers URLs from search** | Guessed domains picked other companies; Clearbit's keyless lookup (exact name match) is accurate. Search in the user's browser finds careers sites the scripts can't; scripted DuckDuckGo and LinkedIn company pages refuse automated use. |
| **`coverage.py` keeps a per-company status** | Every bank company gets a status: source with/without fitting roles, nothing to read, blocked, dead/retryable, or pending, so gaps are measured instead of guessed. |
| **Discovery renders careers pages in headless Chrome** | Plain HTML of modern careers pages rarely names the job platform (the list is built by JavaScript); rendering found boards the plain fetch missed. Bounded regexes keep huge pages fast. |
| **LinkedIn public guest search as the second source** | Covers companies with no readable board (Flipkart, Meta, Netflix, Zepto…). In a measured run it supplied 6 of the top 10 roles. |
| **Two speeds** (`--sources fast` vs `linkedin`) | LinkedIn must be slow (rate limits). Company boards give a usable dashboard in ~1 min, and LinkedIn fills in every ~40 postings. |
| **One raw file per source** | A slow or failed source never empties the results; it keeps its last good data. `--rescore` re-filters without the network. |
| **LinkedIn light mode by default**, `--deep` optional | India-wide role searches plus a 40-company sweep give nearly all LinkedIn value. Per-city searches and big sweeps added ~5 roles for hours of crawling. |
| **The company bank never drops a company for being quiet** | Hiring is cyclical. Each run re-checks every company, and `bank-history.json` shows when a quiet one starts hiring. |
| **Title filter: every software-development role at the user's level; only non-development work and off-profile tracks are excluded** (`profile.json` `title`) | Rebuilt 15 Nov 2026 after audits of 15,380 real India titles, a role-by-role audit against the user's CV, and five independent review rounds. Policy (user): never drop a development role whatever the spelling (MTS, FDE, SDE in any team, backend, full stack, frontend, mobile, .NET, "<language> developer"); a few wrong extras are acceptable. Order in `title_family`: `exclude` on the whole title (seniority and levels, intern/contract, hard non-development types: SRE/DevOps, data engineering, QA/SDET, recruiters, writers, support/production support) → `exclude_head` (manager, lead, director, architect, test, security engineer, administration, disputes…: words that also name teams or products) checked only against the title's head when the head is a core title ("SWE II, Ads Manager" stays, "Engineering Manager" goes) → `exclude_domain` (support, hardware design) unless a developer marker or a core title → `include` (core) → `exclude_unless_core` → `families` (generic, ai; infra and data off) with the JD check. Title numerals III/3 (`exclude_level`) are decided in `evaluate()` by the JD's stated years: Google's "Software Engineer III" asks 1–2 yrs. Every rule is a JSON list of alternatives, compiled in code (`_rx`): string edits had silently deleted rules three times. Title rejections go to `rejected-titles-<date>.csv` for audits. |
| **Profile fit and JD evidence: tracks that don't transfer are dropped; evidence is tiered and judged by dominance; rules are lists; a golden set of real postings guards every change** (`profile.json` `fit`, `title.software_jd`; `tests/test_golden.py`) | Embedded/firmware/driver/RTOS/chip tooling, CRM/low-code platforms (Salesforce/Apex, ServiceNow, Guidewire configuration, SAP ABAP, Workday, Power Platform; integration tools like Boomi/MuleSoft only when they dominate), QA and pre-sales are separate careers for a Java/Spring + Vue/React profile (user, 15 Nov 2026). `off_profile_track`: a title naming the track drops the role; else the JD must name the role (`jd_role`: "As a QA Engineer…", "3+ years of experience in quality assurance", pre-sales phrases) or carry ≥ `jd_min` track terms that outnumber **application-stack** terms (`fit.app_terms`: web/backend/mobile tech and app languages, counted one by one). Track-neutral words (code review, algorithms, data structures, design patterns) and C/C++/Python appear in every kind of software JD, so they count for neither side; comparing against the whole development vocabulary let embedded roles through, comparing against too narrow a list dropped a Java/Kotlin app role (Renesas). An explicit developer title is dropped as QA only on a role statement (developers write tests too). Backend/full-stack/web/Java titles override tracks; the platform vendor's own engineers skip the CRM JD check. The JD check for titles with no role word needs ≥ 2 development terms, more development than penalty terms, and ≥ 1 strong term (frameworks, services, APIs, app languages, CUDA/vLLM, LLM agents, "design and implement features"); penalties never subtract (one incidental "patching" had cancelled a Spring framework role). A list of bare languages counts as one term. Every regex rule is a JSON list of alternatives joined in code: string surgery had silently deleted rules three times. `tests/fixtures/golden_roles.json` labels ~100 real postings keep/drop (with the expected drop reason); `golden_texts.json` snapshots their JDs (personal copy only), so `tests/test_golden.py` runs them through `evaluate()` even after postings close. |
| **Workday: read every India posting through the board's own India facet** | Keyword searches stopped at 200 results per query worldwide and only found the words typed, so Cisco lost 38 fitting India roles past the cap. The reader now takes the country facet (or every India location value) from the board's facet list and pages through all India postings with empty search text (Workday reports `total` on the first page only). Boards without an India facet fall back to the old keyword searches. |
| **Pay evidence lives in `tiers.json`** | Levels.fyi blocks scripts (403), so an agent looks pay up with WebFetch and records it once; every run reuses it. Total comp, not fixed CTC. An overall-only median ≥ 30 L counts as likely; 20–30 L stays borderline (overall medians lean senior). `below_title` excludes the levels that pay below the floor (e.g. Mastercard SE-I, BlackRock Analyst). |
| **Pay confidence on every role**: Posted › Strong (levels.fyi) › Likely (tier judgement, with the reason) › Unverified (goes to leads) | Your brief allows "Likely" if the reason is stated. Unverified roles never reach the shortlist. |
| **Score weights: pay 35, level fit 30, company 15, stack 10, freshness and link 10** (`profile.json` `score_weights`) | Pay and years asked are prerequisites; a stack can be learned. Stack weighs less than company quality, and less than the gap between Strong and Likely pay evidence (0.3 x 35 = 10.5), so a well-paying role with little stack overlap outranks a perfect stack match whose pay is only a guess. Changed 10 Nov 2026 from stack 35 / level 20 / pay 20 / company 15 / fresh 10, which ranked Google, Microsoft and Amazon SDE roles 70-90th for not naming Java/Spring. |
| **Experience = the smallest "N+ years" / "N–M years" near experience words; years in the title override** | Lenient on purpose ("2+ required, 5+ preferred" counts as 2). A role with no years stated gets a neutral score, not a rejection. |
| **Experience focus: 0–2 years is the sweet spot; campus hiring excluded** | Every role gets an experience band (0–1, 2, 3 yrs, not stated). A role is a *sweet spot* when it asks ≤ 2 years and its pay is backed by salary data (not a tier judgement). Level fit (raw 0-20, scaled to its weight): 1-2 yrs 20, 0 yrs 19, 3 yrs 12. New-grad, graduate, early-career, campus and fresher titles are excluded, as are descriptions that say "campus hiring/drive/placement", "graduating in …", "2026 batch". A duty like "help with campus recruitments" doesn't count (Graviton false positive, fixed with a test). |
| **Exclusions match leading words** ("EY" matches "EY GDS", not "Honeywell"); staffing keywords match whole words anywhere | The old substring match silently dropped Disney+ Hotstar, Honeywell, Palo Alto Networks, Anthropic, Northern Trust… |
| **Company matching: exact name, or a prefix with ≥ 5 characters** | Merges real aliases (AWS → Amazon, DeepMind → Google) without merging different companies (CRED ≠ Credgenics/CredHive). |
| **LinkedIn duplicate = same title on the company's own board (today's listing)** | Some companies post roles on LinkedIn that their board doesn't show (e.g. Clearwater SDE-II); those are kept. |
| **Expiry** (below) | Dead jobs go; older active jobs stay. |
| **Marks live in the browser (localStorage), keyed by job link, plus a company + title + location match for reposts; `job-archive.json` keeps closed roles** | No server needed, and refreshes never lose marks. Export/Import backs them up. |

## Expiry: dead jobs out, older active jobs kept

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Role listed on the company's own board | Kept at any age (the board is authoritative) |
| Role gone from a clean read of the company's board | Removed (closed) |
| A posting's detail call fails (429, 5xx, dropped connection) | Not a closure: only 404/410 means closed. The posting is kept from the list data (`meta.detail = "unread"`, judged with the lenient JD check), its previous full read wins if there is one, and the read ends as `DetailsUnread`. Applies to Workday, Greenhouse, Oracle, Rippling, Eightfold (20 Nov 2026) and SmartRecruiters (21 Nov 2026); an Eightfold PCSX answer with no `data` is read as closed. When the list itself was read completely and only detail calls failed, the read still counts as good (`last_ok` moves on, nothing missing from the list is carried) and the source is noted. Unread postings with a development title (core, AI, "developer") are kept and labelled "description not read"; a generic title waits for a run that reads its description. |
| A board read in full only up to a cap (Workday 3,000, Oracle 3,000, SmartRecruiters 3,000 India postings) | A failed read, so the postings past the cap are carried rather than closed. Oracle and SmartRecruiters list every India posting and filter titles here, not with a keyword search (keywords missed "Engineer II, Software Dev."; SmartRecruiters' 1,000-posting page limit hid most of Bosch's India roles; 21 Nov 2026). An Oracle site over the cap (Kotak, ~11,700, mostly branch banking) is read by ten broad keywords instead, each of which must fit under the cap. SmartRecruiters stops detail calls after 3 failures in a row and keeps the rest from the list, and its stub URL has the same `<id>-<title slug>` form as a full read so the posting keeps its identity |
| Board read failed (error, timeout) | The last good read's postings are carried, labelled "carried", for up to 21 days (`BOARD_CARRY_DAYS`) |
| Board that had 10+ postings suddenly returns under 20% of them (or 3+ postings drop to 0), with no error | Treated as a bad read: previous postings kept and the source flagged "suspect"; after 7 days of the drop (`COLLAPSE_CONFIRM_DAYS`) it is accepted as real |
| LinkedIn role seen again this run | Kept |
| LinkedIn role not seen again | Checked on LinkedIn's public job page (404 or "No longer accepting applications" = closed). Up to `liveness_checks_per_run` per pass, ones about to age out first, rechecked after `liveness_recheck_days` |
| … confirmed open | Kept at any age while the "open" answer is under 90 days old; a posting older than 90 days whose last "open" answer is also older than that needs a fresh check |
| … confirmed closed | Dropped |
| … not yet checked, within `max_age_days` (30) | Kept |
| … not yet checked, past the window (budget ran out or rate limit) | Kept until a check answers, first in line for the next run's checks; only postings older than `unchecked_max_age_days` (90) that were never confirmed open age out. (Until 20 Nov 2026 these were dropped unseen.) |
| Shortlist `listed_open` column | Says how liveness is known: listed in the latest read, carried after a failed read, open on LinkedIn (checked date), or not checked yet |
| Each run | `output/changes-<date>.md/.json` lists roles added, roles that left and why (closed at source, closed on LinkedIn, aged out, now filtered out), source count changes and companies that started or stopped hiring |
| Dashboard | A closed role you marked stays visible, tagged "no longer listed"; unmarked ones are hidden |

## Tried and rejected

| Idea | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **JobSpy** for Naukri, Indeed, Google | Not used. For Naukri it forges the app's anti-bot token, which gets around the reCAPTCHA; for Indeed it uses the mobile app's private API key; for Google it replays internal search parameters. All of these get around access controls. |
| **Naukri, Instahyre, Hirist, Cutshort** | Need a reCAPTCHA or login; not scripted. A possible legitimate route is an importer for result pages the user saves themselves. |
| **Darwinbox** (Zepto, BookMyShow…), **TurboHire** (Flipkart), **Myntra's portal** | Darwinbox's job endpoints return 403 (all tenants since 20 Nov 2026: Cloudflare firewall rule on `/ms/candidateapi/`), TurboHire has no public data source, and Myntra's portal has bot detection. Covered through LinkedIn instead. |
| **Walmart Workday board (old host)** | `wd5` refused requests (422). Discovery found the board on `wd504`, which answers; it is now a direct source. |
| **VC portfolio boards (Getro: Accel, General Catalyst) as a job source** | Job data is public, but jobs from acquired companies are filed under the old startup's name (Salesforce roles as "Airkit", PayPal as "Simility"…), so as a source it adds mislabelled duplicates. **Used for discovery only**: their apply links reveal company boards. |
| **Consider-based VC boards** (Peak XV, Lightspeed, Bessemer) | The search endpoint needs a session token from the page; not used. Their portfolio company names come from the ISJB dataset instead. |
| **LinkedIn company sweep with OR-batched names** | The biggest poster crowds out the rest (Walmart took 24/30 results). One search per company instead, rotating. |
| **LinkedIn "engineering function" filter** | Ignored by the public search. |
| **Hiring.cafe, Apify ATS actors** | No public API (hiring.cafe) or commercial (Apify). |
| **schema.org JobPosting on careers *list* pages** | 0 of 100 sampled list pages had it. Job *detail* pages do (see the sitemap reader). |
| **LinkedIn "apply on company site" links to find boards** | LinkedIn hides them unless signed in. |
| **Wikidata for company websites** | Many Indian companies missing, and it rate-limits; recorded `site` values plus checked domain guesses instead. |
| **Meta's careers GraphQL; Millennium's PCSX search; scripted browsers on challenge pages** (11 Nov 2026) | Meta's search needs a per-page `lsd` token and Millennium's PCSX answers "not enabled"; both are read from their public sitemaps instead. Driving a browser through Cloudflare/PerimeterX pages on a schedule would be getting around bot protection, so those sites stay on LinkedIn. |
| **Navi's "Ashby board"** | The `navi` Ashby board belongs to a US aviation startup, not Navi (India); board names must be checked against the company. Navi's real list is its own careers API (above). |
| **JioStar (Disney+ Hotstar) Workday; Walmart's new careers site** | JioStar's Workday returns 403 to scripts, so it stays on LinkedIn. Walmart's new site ignores the search and lists US store jobs, but its Workday board (`walmart.wd504`) answers again and is read directly. |

## Sources of companies

- Hand-curated tiers: big tech, Indian product companies, fintech, banks, trading firms, R&D centres, SaaS, AI.
- `import_lists.py`: [moreThanFAANGM](https://github.com/Kaustubh-Natuskar/moreThanFAANGM) (MIT, ~480 product companies with careers links) and [ISJB](https://github.com/pHequals7/isjb) (portfolio companies of Accel, Peak XV, Lightspeed, Nexus, Bessemer, General Catalyst, Blume).
- `discover_ats.py --bank` (board-name guessing, with the company name verified), `--careers` (reads boards from careers URLs and VC apply links). Each new board is checked for India roles and the right company before it's added.
- LinkedIn surfaces unknown companies; their roles go to leads until pay is checked.

## Known limits

- Pay figures are levels.fyi **total comp** (base + stock + bonus), not fixed CTC.
- 17 roles from 8 companies rely on tier judgement (no public salary data): IMC, Citadel Securities, NK Securities, Sarvam, Onehouse, ClickHouse, Hevo, Urban Company.
- ~170 of ~300 shortlisted roles ask "3+ years": allowed by the brief (≤ 3), but stretch roles at ~1.5 years. The dashboard's "Sweet spot only" filter shows the 0–2-year roles with evidenced pay.
- Many good companies currently list only senior roles in India (e.g. Coupang 42, Celonis 27, Roku). They stay in the bank and appear when they post SDE-level roles.
- Companies on Darwinbox (403 to scripts), TurboHire (token-gated), and sites blocked by bot protection, are visible only through LinkedIn. Their own boards were checked once in a browser (11 Nov 2026): the blocked ones hold few India software roles (Wayfair 6 jobs, Citadel Securities ~2).
- The sitemap reader reads at most 150 job pages per company per run, chosen by URL wording (software/engineer/developer…, not senior). A site whose job URLs carry no title words (bare IDs) is read only if it has a search feed (e.g. Jibe).
- **Darwinbox** (43 companies: Zepto, Porter, Rapido, KreditBee, Moneyview…): since ~15–20 Nov 2026 a Cloudflare firewall rule answers 403 to scripts on every `/ms/candidateapi/` path of every tenant (the careers pages themselves still load). All 40 that aren't below the pay bar or non-software businesses are searched on LinkedIn every run (`linkedin_every_run`); don't work around the block.
- **Workday throttling:** all `*.myworkdayjobs.com` tenants share one Cloudflare edge, so parallel boards add up. At most 3 Workday boards are read at once (`SHARED_HOST_SLOTS`); a detail call that still gets a 429 keeps the posting from the list data (`detail = "unread"`); the read still counts as good because the listing was complete. A failed detail call on a multi-location posting (no India location in the list data) marks the listing incomplete, so the last good postings are carried. (Until 20 Nov 2026 a failed detail call counted as a closed posting, which made Visa's board read as cleanly empty.)
- **Direct boards that read empty or fail stay in the LinkedIn company-sweep pool** (since 20 Nov 2026): only a board that returned postings replaces the company's LinkedIn search, so a silently broken reader can't hide a company.
- Goldman Sachs and Juspay roles have no posting date in their feeds (freshness gets a neutral score). Juspay's links open the careers page, not the single role.
- LinkedIn liveness checks are budgeted; under heavy rate limiting, old postings wait (kept, labelled unchecked) until a later run checks them.

## Tests

`tests/test_sources.py` covers the Oracle, Eightfold and Rippling readers, every `portals/` reader (including the sitemap page cache, longest-match robots.txt rules, unreadable-sitemap failures, `india_in_url`, Navi's unwrapped TurboHire list and Zerodha), board recognition and the discovery helpers, including two regex-speed regressions that once froze discovery. Its weaker checks were found by mutation testing and strengthened. The checks added on 11 Nov 2026 were mutation-tested after a review found three robots.txt bugs that the first version of the suite let through (tie-breaking, the group for our user agent, `find_careers.py` ignoring robots.txt); 13 reintroduced bugs now each fail a named check. `tests/test_titles.py` holds the title-audit cases (titles wrongly dropped before, titles that must stay out), the JD software check and the Workday India-facet reader. `tests/test_pipeline.py` also checks that `linkedin_every_run` companies are searched every run. The older fixture-based suites run against a temp folder (never real data), and each was mutation-tested: every fixed bug was reintroduced and the suite failed. They cover filters, Greenhouse and Workday fetching (failures don't abort a board), duplicate detection against today's boards, aliases, the concurrency of the board-title table, company matching, exclusions, LinkedIn expiry and state merging, board-link recognition, and the importer's exclusion rule.
